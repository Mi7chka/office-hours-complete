SAVE GREENLINE {{VERSION}} FOR MAC
The Office Hours game, from Mitchell B Consulting


TO INSTALL

1. Drag "Save Greenline" into your Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click Save Greenline.
3. macOS will stop it the first time. See THE WARNING below. You do this once.


THE WARNING, AND HOW TO ALLOW THE APP

The first time you open it, macOS shows a box that says something like:

   "Save Greenline" Not Opened
   Apple could not verify "Save Greenline" is free of malware that may harm
   your Mac or compromise your privacy.

Click Done. Do not click Move to Trash. Then:

1. Open the Apple menu (top left of the screen) and choose System Settings.
2. Click Privacy & Security in the list on the left.
3. Scroll down to the part called Security. It says:
   "Save Greenline" was blocked to protect your Mac.
4. Click Open Anyway.
5. Click Open Anyway again, then type your Mac password or use Touch ID.

The game opens. From then on it opens with a double-click like any other app.
The Open Anyway button only shows for about an hour after you try to open the
app. If it is not there, double-click Save Greenline again and look again.

On an older Mac (macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier) there is a shorter way: in
Applications, hold the Control key and click Save Greenline, choose Open, then
click Open in the box that appears.

Why you see it: this app is not signed and not notarized. That means its maker
has not paid Apple for a developer ID and has not sent the app to Apple to be
checked. macOS shows this warning for every app like that. The exact words
change a little from one version of macOS to the next.


WHAT YOU NEED

macOS 11 or newer, and a web browser. With Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
installed, the game opens in a window of its own. With neither, it opens in
your usual browser (Safari, for example) as an ordinary tab.

While the game is open in its own window, the Dock shows a second Chrome (or
Edge) icon for it. To quit, press Command and Q, as with any Mac app.


YOUR PROGRESS

Your progress is saved on this Mac only. It does not follow you to another
computer, and the game does not go online or send anything anywhere. It is
also separate from the web version of the game: progress made in one does not
show in the other.


TO UPDATE

Download the new copy and drag it into Applications. When the Mac asks, choose
Replace. Your progress is kept. macOS will ask you to allow the new copy, the
same way as the first time.


TO REMOVE

Drag Save Greenline from Applications to the Trash.
To remove your saved progress as well: in Finder, open the Go menu, choose
Go to Folder, paste the line below, press Return, and move the folder called
Save Greenline to the Trash.

   ~/Library/Application Support
