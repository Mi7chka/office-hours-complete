# Week 6 · three checks for the content calendar and the posts board

Write the checks before the build. Run them with the sample business. Tick a check only when you saw it with your own eyes.

1. **The calendar and the board agree.** The Posts tab shows the number 9. Count the posts on the Calendar: there are 9, each on its own day, and today is marked.
2. **Posted is a tick a person makes.** On the Posts tab, tick Posted on a card under Ready. It moves to the Posted column, and the number on the home screen goes down by one.
3. **The invented claim can be stopped.** In Content pack, load the sample answer. Find the option that starts with Voted the best landscaper, and press Something here is not true. It is crossed out, and its Use this one button is gone.
