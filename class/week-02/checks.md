# Week 2 · three checks for the board and the change log

Write the checks before the build. Run them with the sample business. Tick a check only when you saw it with your own eyes.

1. **The count matches.** Open the board and count the lines under Waiting on you. The number is the same as the number on the board's tile on the home screen.
2. **The step with no owner is marked.** The card Fix the trailer hitch light has a red label No owner. Choose You for that step, and the label goes away.
3. **Done writes the log.** Move the card Order mulch for Friday's jobs to Done. Open the Change log tab. The top line starts with Done and has today's date.
